## About me

I’m **Tabish Javed**, a full-stack web developer and Computer Science & Engineering graduate from **Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University**. In my first professional role, I independently worked on live websites and web experiences for education, beauty, events, real estate, and employee verification.

I enjoy turning real requirements into polished, responsive products—whether that’s a company website, a campaign landing page, a booking experience, or a platform with many supporting pages.

> **What I bring:** end-to-end ownership, practical problem-solving, and a focus on clear, user-friendly web experiences.
